- RE-ENGINEERED CONTINUOUS ALIGNMENT METRIC (Wang & Isola, 2020 generalization):
  * Replaced batch-top-10% percentile alignment with row-normalized expected cosine similarity:
    Align_sim = (1/B) * sum_{i=1}^B sum_{j != i} w_norm_{ij} * cos(z_i, z_j)  where  w_norm_{ij} = w_{ij} / sum_{k != i} w_{ik}
  * Eliminates global sum bias (preventing stationary vowels/silence from dominating aggregate sums) and guarantees per-instance expectation, preserving mathematical duality with Wang & Isola squared distance.
